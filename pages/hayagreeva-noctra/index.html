Hayagreeva HEx

A liquid-cooling powerhouse. The cold plate mounts directly on a GPU, CPU, or accelerator package. Liquid transports thermal energy to a coolant distribution unit (CDU), then to the facility heat-rejection system — where air cooling can no longer keep pace with rack power density.

Chip → Hayagreeva HEx → CDU → Facility

HEx product

Validated for continuous heat removal above 5,000 W — built for high-density AI racks and OEM platforms.

01 Thermal capacity

More than 5,000 W continuous heat removal at 0.005 °C/W rated thermal resistance. At 5,000 W the measured path implies roughly a 25 °C rise — exact results depend on coolant, flow rate, inlet temperature, and package geometry.

02 Copper matrix

A copper matrix with more than 4,000 m²/m³ of wetted surface area expands the heat-transfer interface at the chip boundary — engineered for extreme heat flux at the package.

Energy path

Chip to facility

Hayagreeva HEx sits where semiconductor waste heat becomes manageable fluid energy. Thermal load moves off the die, through the rack manifold and CDU, and out of the building — enabling sustained accelerator performance without thermal throttling.

  • Lower chip temperature — less throttling under load
  • System-scale efficiency — pressure drop and pump energy across the loop
  • Facility integration — CDUs, manifolds, building heat rejection
