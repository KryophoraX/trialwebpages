// FAQ

Common questions

What is Hayagreeva HEx?

HEx is the Hayagreeva Exchange — a direct-to-chip cold plate that removes heat at the semiconductor package and passes it into the facility fluid path.

What capacity does HEx support?

Continuous heat removal above 5,000 W at a rated thermal resistance of 0.005 °C/W, with copper matrix surface density above 4,000 m²/m³.

Which accelerators are supported?

Package-specific fitting for NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel platforms — mounting, connectors, and fluid interfaces matched to each package.

When does HEx launch?

Product launch is May 28, 2026. Evaluation requests are open now for data centers, OEMs, integrators, and hyperscale programs.
